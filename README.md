# eCPR Express

California certified payroll, in minutes — free in-browser generator for:

- **DIR eCPR XML** — schema-valid upload files for California's electronic certified payroll reporting (no more hand-keying every worker, every week, or editing XML in Notepad)
- **Official WH-347 PDF** — the genuine U.S. DOL form, filled and totaled, including the Statement of Compliance

**Use it now: https://jacob-pv.github.io/ecpr-express/**

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Names, SSNs, and wages are stored in your browser's local storage and the files you download — nothing is ever sent to a server.

## Who it's for

Small contractors and subcontractors on California public-works jobs (and their bookkeepers) who need weekly certified payroll without enterprise software prices.
