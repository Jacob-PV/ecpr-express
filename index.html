FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes — free while we're in launch. When paid plans start, early users keep a founding discount. Email us to lock it in.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. The app is a static page; all data lives in your browser's local storage and the files you download. We never see your payroll.

Does this submit to the DIR for me?

Not yet. You download the XML file and upload it at the DIR's eCPR page (Submit Payroll in XML File), then sign and submit there. That upload takes about a minute — the painful part (typing in every worker, every day, every deduction) is what we eliminate.

Is the WH-347 accepted by agencies?

We fill the official U.S. Department of Labor WH-347 form itself (OMB-approved layout), including the Statement of Compliance on page 2.

What about more than 8 workers?

The WH-347 has 8 rows per sheet — we automatically produce additional continuation sheets in the same PDF. The eCPR XML has no worker limit.
