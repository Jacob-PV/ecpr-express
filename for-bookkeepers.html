Built for multi-client work

  • One profile per client. Set up a client's company, projects, crew, and rates once. Export it as a backup file; keep one file per client. Restore a client's file, enter the week's hours, download their eCPR XML + WH-347, move to the next client.
  • The math is done and checked. Totals, gross this-project vs all-work, deductions, net wages — calculated and validated against the DIR's schema before anything is uploaded. Arithmetic errors are the #1 reason filings get flagged.
  • Nothing to install, nothing to subscribe per client. It's a browser page. Client SSNs and wages stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded to us, which keeps your engagement letter's confidentiality promises intact.
  • Non-performance weeks in one click — keep every client's payroll numbering unbroken without rebuilding a report.

Why this beats the alternatives

Enterprise certified-payroll platforms run $99–300/month and are priced per contractor — absurd for a bookkeeper with six small subs as clients.
